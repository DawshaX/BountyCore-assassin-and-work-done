Quick start
-----------
1. Open FAB-ANSWERS.txt on your phone and fill the form top to bottom.
2. Upload thumbnail first, then the 7 gallery images in the listed order.
3. Add product zip via "Add new format".
4. Work through the checklist at the bottom of FAB-ANSWERS.txt, then
   Submit for review.
